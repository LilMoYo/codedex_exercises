Spider-Man

Spider-Man is the secret identity of Peter Benjamin Parker. He was raised by his Aunt May and Uncle Ben in Queens, New York City, after his parents Richard and Mary Parker were killed in a plane crash. Peter was bitten by a radioactive spider, which gave him superhuman strength, agility, and the ability to cling to walls. After the death of his Uncle Ben, Peter vowed to use his powers to fight crime and protect the innocent.
