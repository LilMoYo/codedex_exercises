AROUND THE WORLD; 7 Are Killed and 21 Hurt In Fighting in Beirut

Special to The New York Times

A second day of fierce fighting erupted today between Lebanon's rival Moslem and Christian factions in Beirut and the eastern mountains, and the police said 7 people were killed and 21 wounded.

Meanwhile Katyusha rockets struck the headquarters of the Israeli-backed South Lebanon Army inside Israel's so-called security zone in southern Lebanon.

In Beirut, mortar shells struck populated areas on both sides of the Green Line that divides the city into Christian and Moslem sectors.

There was no explanation for the sudden flare-up of violence.
