Artist Name: BABYMETAL

BABYMETAL is a Japanese "Kawaii Metal" band formed in 2010, famous for pioneering a unique fusion of heavy metal and J-pop. Led by the "Fox God," the group has gained international acclaim for their high-energy performances, intricate choreography, and powerful vocals, breaking boundaries across genres and cultures.
